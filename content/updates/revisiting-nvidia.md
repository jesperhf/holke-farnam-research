### November 12, 2026

**Jensen Huang resigned as CEO and from the board, effective today.** CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and remains CFO until a permanent CEO is named, and the board will consider internal and external candidates. Harvey Jones, a director since 1993 and former CEO of Synopsys, becomes Nvidia's first board chair. The board said it expects operations, customer commitments and the product roadmap "to continue without interruption," and the November 17 report is unchanged ([Nvidia](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition)). The release does not mention findings from the board's investigation. The stock was down about 13% before the open, near $150.71 against a $173.73 close.

**What I changed in the model.** The entry above treated Huang leaving as a risk worth 2% to 5% of value. It has now happened, so it goes into the cash flows instead of a probability:

- **Fiscal 2027 and 2028 are unchanged.** That revenue is largely contracted and limited by supply, and the roadmap is set years ahead.
- **The long run is weaker in the base case.** Operating margin is 1 point lower in fiscal 2029 and 2 points lower after that, and fiscal 2030 to 2031 revenue is 5% lower, for a slower response to custom chips without the founder who built the ecosystem. The terminal margin falls from 46% to 44%.
- **The bull case takes a smaller version of the same cut**, 3% lower revenue in fiscal 2030 to 2031 and margins 1 to 2 points lower. The bear case, which is about a capex digestion, is unchanged.
- **The discount rate is unchanged.** Leadership uncertainty belongs in the cash flows, not the rate.

It is just above the new $130 exit level, which means a fall to $135 would bring the thesis review close behind it; any add there waits for the November 17 report to hold up.

**The market cut more than I did.** My base value fell 8%; the stock fell about 13%, roughly $556 billion of market value. At today's price the base case earns 11.1% a year against the 10.6% I require: slightly undervalued on my numbers, with more uncertainty than when I wrote the entry. I am holding, and I will add only below $135.
